Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave must be requested at least 5 days in advance via the HR portal. Unused leave can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Medical leave requires a doctor's certificate for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days. Public holidays are separate from annual leave entitlements.

Metformin is a first-line medication for Type 2 diabetes. It works by reducing glucose production in the liver and improving insulin sensitivity. Common side effects include nausea, diarrhea, and stomach upset, usually improving after a few weeks. It should be taken with meals.
